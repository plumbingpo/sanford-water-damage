Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Long-running hidden leaks, for example at a dishwasher line, sink drain or roof flashing
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Feeling better away from home, then having coughs, headaches or allergies come back indoors
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Discolored or darkened spots near HVAC vents, across ceilings or on walls

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Regular filter changes and HVAC maintenance help keep mold out of the ducts
  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule

DIY vs. Professional Mold Remediation

Some simple tasks are suitable for DIY, but most mold work benefits from professional tools, training, and experience. Consider the following comparison:

Factor DIY Approach Professional Service
Health Safety Scrubbing sends harmful spores airborne Full containment and PPE prevent exposure
Cross-Contamination Clean rooms pick up drifting spores Negative air containment prevents spread

Debris Disposal Moldy debris dragged through clean rooms Debris bagged inside containment before removal

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.
